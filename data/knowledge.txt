
# Company BP QA
Company BP QA validates company-level machine learning features.
It checks missing values, data types, numerical ranges,
and product-specific business rules.

# Contact BP QA
Contact BP QA validates contact-level machine learning features.
It checks non-null identifiers, feature and target columns,
boolean values, and ratios between zero and one.

# Hexagon Scoring
Hexagon combines activity-level signals into a predictive score.
It uses activity probabilities and time-dependent weights.
A sample combination formula is:
score = 1 - product((1 - p_i) ** w_i)

# QA Troubleshooting
When a QA validation fails, investigate the organization,
product, source data, failing SQL, null values,
schema changes, and validation thresholds.
